# PROVENANCE — hero image prompts

Art direction: PROVENANCE is the institutional flagship — NOT REPLAY's orange quirk.
Palette: obsidian black, dark slate, engraved GOLD/brass, thin proof-green accent only.
Mood: ratings-agency gravitas meets crypto-native precision. Forensic, monumental, sober.
Bans: people, coins, generic blockchain cubes/nodes, purple AI gradients, robots.
All 16:9 unless noted. Generator: nano_banana_pro (MCP) or ChatGPT images.

## 1. The Grade Seal (primary hero)
Monumental cinematic hero image for PROVENANCE, an AI ratings agency for tokenized
real-world assets. A massive embossed metallic gold rating seal stamped with the letters
"AA" floats above a dark polished obsidian surface, fine engraved concentric rings and
micro-etched ledger text circling the seal, thin gold light tracing the engraving, faint
columns of financial data and hash characters etched subtly into the black stone below,
deep charcoal-black background, dramatic rim lighting, institutional gravitas meets
crypto-native precision, premium financial editorial photography, no people, no coins,
no generic blockchain cubes, 16:9.
